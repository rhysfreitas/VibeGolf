# Wrecker's Reach: art brief (art pass 1)

Rhys asked for the art pass on 15 November 2026, after the dune grass review ("I think this looks good"). His references
come first: `refs/dunes-marram.png` (marram clumps on pale rippled sand, sea beyond) and `refs/dunes-fence-storm.png`
(a storm clearing over breakers, low sun through the cloud, dune grass backlit, a weathered post fence on the dune).
The concept chat's vegetation addendum adds his original "seaside dunes" photo: big separate tussocks 1 to 1.5 m,
straw gold to yellow-green, darker olive at the base, rim-lit in low sun, bare pale sand between.

## The feel

A wild links the morning after a wrecking gale, seen late in the afternoon as the storm clears out to sea: wet,
washed and glowing. Grand and a little melancholy, never spooky; the wrecks are history, not horror.

## Materials

- **Dunes:** pale sand with wind ripples about 12 cm apart, lying across the sea wind (from the south-west), softened
  in the hollows; darker damp sand on the north faces and under the grass.
- **Beach:** wet sand, glossy near the water (lower roughness, catching the sky), a wrack line of weed and driftwood
  along the top of the wet band.
- **Turf:** firm links grass, more gold than green, faint mowing stripes; fescue wispy, gold, with sand showing.
- **Pots:** revetted sod faces, turf layers about 15 cm thick stacked up the face, a lip of grass over them.
- **Wrecks:** planked hulls (planks about 25 cm wide), ribs about 30 cm thick at 60 cm centres where the planking is
  gone (the Florida and Nantucket beach wrecks), silver-grey where dry, dark and weed-green below the high-water line,
  treenails and iron strapping. Masts dark, salt-stained.
- **Buildings:** the Wrecker's Inn in whitewashed rubble stone with a slate roof and a stern castle built into it;
  the beacon tower in grey stone with a lantern room; luggers hauled up, tarred black with a coloured strake.
- **Sea:** a swell running in from the west, white surf lines on the beaches and a line of breakers out on the
  sandbar that wrecks the ships; the lagoon calm and mirror-like; the Cauldron greener and choppy.

## Vegetation

Marram is the signature (done in the dune grass round; it should move in the wind now). Colour in small doses:
pink thrift and blue sea holly in the fescue, yellow gorse on the landward side and round the inn, reeds along the
lagoon and the creek. No trees.

## Sky, light and weather

The storm clearing late in the afternoon: the sun low in the west over the sea (about 8 to 12°), a dark storm deck
retreating east over the lagoon, broken cumulus with gold edges overhead and rays of sunlight fanning down over the
sea (Rhys's fence photo). The sun backlights the grass on holes that play west, rim-lit and glowing. Everything still
damp: a sheen on the sand, the turf a little darker than dry.

## Life and details

A few, off the lines: gulls wheeling and perched on the wrecks, oystercatchers on the beach, seals hauled out on the
sandbar, washed-up barrels, spars and rope, a weathered post fence on the dunes (his photo), lobster pots by the
luggers, the false light's lantern glowing, the beacon lit, smoke from the inn's chimney, a rowing boat on the
lagoon.

## Landmarks

The beacon tower on the point (the 5th's reveal), the galleon on her side (the 3rd), the frigate's masts in the
Cauldron, the inn at the end.

## Avoid

Tropical turquoise water and white beaches, palms, bright saturated turf, cartoon pirates (skulls, treasure chests),
anything modern, busy props.

## Rhys's picks (15 November 2026)

1. Wrecks and buildings: **silver-grey weathered** (planked hulls and bare ribs in salt-silvered timber, dark and
   weed-green below the high-water line, iron straps; the inn whitewashed with a slate roof; the beacon grey stone
   with a lit lantern room).
2. Vegetation and ground: **marram plus small colour** (the marram swaying, wind ripples, a wrack line, thrift and sea
   holly in the fescue, gorse inland and round the inn, reeds by the lagoon and the creek).
3. Light and sky: **the storm clearing** (the sun about 10° in the west over the sea, a dark deck retreating east over
   the lagoon, broken gold-edged cumulus, rays fanning over the sea, a wet sheen, the grass backlit).
4. Details: **all four** (gulls and oystercatchers; surf, the breakers on the sandbar and seals hauled out on it;
   flotsam, the dune fence and lobster pots; the false light and the beacon lit, smoke from the inn, a rowing boat).
