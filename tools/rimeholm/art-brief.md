# Rimeholm: art brief

Written for art pass 1 (10 November 2026) from research, since there are no reference photos yet (see
References). The direction gets agreed in four picker questions (`docs/ART-PASS.md`), and Rhys's answers
are recorded at the end.

**Feel.** A still, clear night on a glacier in the far north. Cold and huge, but welcoming: moonlit snow
bright enough to read every lie, the aurora folding overhead, and warm islands of green and firelight
along the lines of play. Awe, not menace.

**Materials**, at real scale
- *Snow*: sastrugi, wind-carved ridges steep on the windward side and tailing off downwind, a few
  centimetres to half a metre high and a few metres long, all lined up with the prevailing wind; drifts
  and scoured blue patches at tens of metres. Moonlight from one side gives each ridge a lit face and a
  blue shadow: that's what keeps the snow from reading flat.
- *Fairways*: groomed snow, the groomer's passes about 5 m wide as mown stripes, fine corduroy across
  them that fades out beyond 30 m.
- *Ice*: blue that deepens with thickness (cobalt to dark turquoise), white and bubbly near the surface;
  faces banded in layers half a metre to two metres thick; crevasse walls a white lip and cornice, blue,
  then black.
- *Rock*: black basalt. On 6, the cliff in columns half a metre to a metre across, like Svartifoss, with
  the frozen falls pouring down them. Erratic boulders dark with snow on their tops.
- *Frozen falls*: ropey white-blue columns flaring at the foot, icicle fringes, a skirt of ice.
- *Travertine*: scalloped rims to the terraces, cream with rust stains where the hot water runs.
- *The hall*: a Norse longhouse about 35 m long and 7 m wide, the long walls bowed out like a hull,
  turf walls a metre or more thick on a stone footing, a turf roof under snow, timber gables with carved
  heads crossing at the ridge, a lit door, smoke from the roof.

**Vegetation.** Almost none on the ice. Round each green and pool, warm moss, grass and dwarf willow;
stunted, snow-laden spruce only in the lower valleys and round the springs.

**Sky, light and weather.** Signature: a clear night, moon about 25° up, cool blue-white light with long
blue shadows. The aurora in bands with folded, rippling lower edges, green with a violet hem and rays
reaching up into faint red; brightest over the 9th. Stars. The greens lit; braziers throw warm pools of
light on the snow along the lines; steam lit from below near the fires.

**Life and details.** A few, kept off the lines: an arctic fox or two, ravens, a distant herd. Spindrift
blowing low over the ice. Norse touches: runestones, carved tee markers.

**Landmarks.** The hall on its ridge (from 7, 8 and 9); the seracs at the icefall (from 4 and 5); 6's
black cliff and frozen falls; the moulin's blue depth; the nunataks round the horizon.

**Avoid.** Flat white snow with no shadow shape; saturated cartoon cyan; a night so dark you can't read
the lie; fantasy creatures; more than the two real lights a hole (bake the rest).

## Agreed (10 November 2026)

| Question | Rhys's pick |
|---|---|
| Ice and rock | **Blue ice and basalt**: glacier blue deepening to black in the crevasses, black basalt (6's cliff in columns, the peaks, erratics) |
| Vegetation | **Warm oases**: moss, grass and dwarf willow round each green and hot pool, thinning out into the snow; snow on the spruce |
| Light and sky | **Moonlit, lively aurora**: a low moon, blue shadows, two folding curtains (green, violet hem, red tops), brightest over the 9th |
| Details | **All four**: lantern cairns along the lines, Norse touches (runestones, carved tee posts, the hall's door and smoke, the longship), blowing spindrift, arctic animals (a fox, a reindeer herd, ravens), all kept off the lines of play |

What went in, pass 1: sastrugi and drifts in the snow shader (lit faces, blue lee shadows, glints close by), groomed
fairway stripes and corduroy, crevasse walls with a cornice, firn, a dirt band and blues going black, basalt in
Voronoi columns on rock faces, braziers and lantern cairns as warm light pools painted into the ground (baked, not
real lights), moss painted round the greens and hot pools with tufts and willow on it, the night sky dome with
stars and the moon, the aurora, spindrift streaks, the animals, runestones and stained tee posts.

**Follow-up (11 November):** Rhys couldn't see the longship (a shadow under the ice, hidden by the 3rd's bluff). It is
now a wreck: the stern frozen in below, the bow rearing out of the ice at 34° for 17 m about 40 m right of the line,
far enough out that the dragon prow shows over the bluff from the tee. Clinker hull in weathered timber, the dragon
head with an open jaw, a snapped mast with a scrap of red-and-cream sail frozen stiff, painted shields along the
gunwales, snow and icicles, the ice heaved up in plates where it broke through, a faint warm glow on it. Solid for
the ball; only a big slice or a ball skating across the ice reaches it.
