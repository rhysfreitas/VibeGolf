# The Aerie: art brief

Researched for its first art pass (10 November 2026; no reference photos yet), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** High, calm and bright: a links-like course on islands of pale sandstone that float above a sea
of cloud in the early morning. The real-world model is Zhangjiajie's quartz-sandstone pillars (the
"Avatar mountains"), where fog fills the gorges and the pillar tops stand in clear air, ancient trees
root in the cracks, and early light turns the towers amber. Fantasy, but grounded in that.

**Palette** (the big surfaces first)

| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Sandstone | `#cdbfa3` `#b39f80` `#d9b98a` | pale quartz sandstone, amber bands where the low sun catches it |
| Rock shadow | `#6f6658` | the undersides and fissures, so islands read solid |
| Moss and cliff plants | `#4f6b30` `#3c5527` | ledges, cracks, hanging growth |
| Pines | `#2c4428` `#38532f` | wind-bent pines on tops and ledges |
| Rough | `#5a7a34` | longer, a little golden in the light |
| Fairway / green | `#5f9a36` / `#6fae40` | mown, clearly lighter than the rough |
| Cloud sea | `#f4f1ea` with `#b9c4cf` shadows | a bright floor below, blue-grey in its folds |
| Sky | `#8fb7de` to `#e9ddc9` at the horizon | clear above, warm haze at the horizon in the morning |

**Materials.** Sandstone in horizontal bands (amber and pale quartz), vertical fractures and straight-edged
faces, dark water streaks down from ledges; undersides darker and rougher than the tops. Grass that
goes on over the rim a little before the rock shows (already there).

**Vegetation.** Pines clinging to tops, ledges and cracks, some leaning out over the drop; moss and
hanging plants down the faces and under the islands; low shrubs along the rims. Fewer generic round
trees.

**Sky, light and weather.** Signature: morning, calm, clear. The sun low enough to light the pillars
amber on one side; a bright cloud sea below that you can read from the tee (a white floor, not a grey
haze); a few high cumulus; mist drifting through the gaps between islands.

**Rhys's picks (10 November 2026).** Golden morning over a bright cloud sea. Plus clouds at and above the
horizon in the background (banks of cumulus standing up behind the far islands), and some in the
foreground too: wisps and small clouds drifting between and in front of the near islands.

**Life and details.** Swifts or a pair of eagles riding the air between islands; prayer-flag or
lantern strings on a footbridge; the balloon; waterfalls falling into the cloud with spray where they
meet it. A few, not busy.

**Landmarks.** The great floating island over the 1st, the balloon, the clubhouse island on the 9th
(seen from several holes).

**Avoid.** Uniform carpet green; every pillar the same tapered shape and colour; a grey horizon where
the cloud sea should be; cartoon trees.
