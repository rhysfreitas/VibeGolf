# Review tools

Headless tools for checking a course against `docs/COURSE-DESIGN.md` and for making review books. They
work on any course module (`aerie`, `redmesa`, `hollowmere`, `reclaim`) and serve the site straight from the repo, so
no web server is needed. Output goes to `tools/review/out/` (ignored by git).

| Tool | What it does | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `node tools/review/analyse.mjs <course>` | Each hole against the rules (with side slope and green contour: each landing area's lean, each green's typical slope, shape beyond its plane, pin and pin areas): height change, bends (+ = right), carries, fairway width where a drive lands, what surrounds each green, steep steps beside fairway, targets. Then the outline checks (`outlines.mjs`, from the shape review): the green's size, depth against width, how close it is to a plain oval and its angle to the approach (L/R); fairway width along the line and how much it changes; each bunker's size, job and whether it touches the fairway; the fairway where each tee set's drive lands (toward its first target, as auto-aim plays); and for the course, green styles, angled greens each way and the par 3s' plays-like spread. Lines starting `!` break a rule | seconds |
| `node tools/review/drives.mjs [course...]` | Tour, medium and shorter drives from the back and second tees at each par 4/5's first target, with the game's physics. Catches walls and water in the landing area | seconds |
| `node tools/review/sample.mjs <course>` then `python3 tools/review/plans.py <course>` | A plan of each hole (tee at the bottom, surface colours, hillshade, 2 m contours, targets) with its profile. Red marks a step of 3 m or more within 8 m of short grass: the plateau check | seconds |
| `node tools/review/slots.mjs <course> [holes]` | Slots: narrow trenches to the floor where two islands meet without overlapping, with playing ground on both sides (a ball falls in). Some are meant (the tip of a canyon you carry); look at each | under a minute |
| `node tools/review/views.mjs <course> [holes] [--only=tee,land,green] [--pose=...]` | Clean in-game renders from the back tee, the landing area and short of the green, plus any extra pose | 1–2 min a hole |
| `node tools/review/playthrough.mjs <course> [holes] [--render=N]` | Plays each hole on auto-aim at phone size and logs the lie after every stroke. A hole that never finishes is a layout bug. `--render=120` renders every 120th step instead of every 6th: the same golf in about a tenth of the time | 3–8 min a hole (under 1 with `--render=120`) |
| `node tools/review/games.mjs [game ...] [--only=play,clean,wide,flight,land] [--tag=x]` | Renders of the mini games for an art review: the play view with its UI, the same view clean, a wide view (the camera held a few metres back and up), the ball in flight after an Ideal shot and the payoff a few seconds later. Writes `out/game_<game>_<view>.png`; `sheet.py --files` makes a sheet of them | about 1–2.5 min a game |
| `node tools/review/quickplay.mjs <course> [holes] [--seeds=3]` | A play-through with the game's physics and no browser: from the back and forward tees, each stroke picks its target as auto-aim does, solves the club and swing length and hits it with a decent amateur's miss; two putts on the green, a stroke for the void or water. Skips lie costs, stances, branches and wind, so it's a check, not the game: use it after any layout change, and `playthrough.mjs` for the holes that changed | 1–2 min a course |
| `node tools/review/design.mjs <course ...\|all> [--seeds=12] [--no-play] [--only=decisions]` | The checks behind the course card and the nine in `docs/COURSE-DESIGN.md`. **Skeleton**: par order, length, net height and bend of every hole, and with more than one course where they repeat each other (`all` adds the planned courses from their plan data). **Rhythm**: par-4 and par-3 spreads, compass quarters, bends each way. **View**: from the stance camera on the back tee, whether the tee shot's target is in the fixed frame and in sight (a blind tee shot is ground 1.5 m or more into the sight line). **Carry**: the longest forced carry on each tee set's line against 60% of its drive. **Gate**: every hole played from the back tees and, by a player 80% as long, from the forward tees; fails a hole that doesn't finish or plays worse than +2. **Decisions**: for each hole with a `bold` target (or one bold by its name, on a course that marks none), what the bold line gains and the strokes each route takes for a long and a short hitter; a bold target out of reach from the tee is scored as a second-shot choice (the drive plays safe, the second goes at it, against the nearest safe lay-up), and a `Green` target marked `bold` is a drivable green; use `--only=decisions --seeds=24`, since at 6 seeds a difference under 0.3 is noise | seconds with `--no-play`; 5–15 min a course with the play |
| `node tools/review/alike.mjs <course ...\|all> [--pairs]` | Are the fairways the same shape? Walks every par 4/5 fairway along its line (width at each point, where its middle sits, lanes, pieces), names its form (spindle, ribbon, fan, funnel, hourglass, bowl, hook, scalloped, split, island, broken, stepping stones) and compares every pair, on the course and across courses. Flags a form more than twice, two in a row, more than two plain strips, and any pair alike (under 0.25). Planned courses are read from `tools/<course>/holes.mjs`. On a generated course (every hole has a `frame`) it counts only a hole's own ground, the points nearer its line than any other hole's, since each field also holds its neighbours' fairways. `docs/COURSE-DESIGN.md` "Sameness" | seconds |
| `node tools/review/fairway-sheet.mjs out.svg` | One white sheet of every par 4/5 fairway on the playable courses, straightened along its playing line and named by `alike.mjs` (red = a plain strip). For before-and-after proposals | seconds |
| `node tools/review/tips.mjs <course ...\|all>` | The tips tee as a switched-off option: is `COURSE.tipsSet` there, has every hole a `tips: { tee, yards }`, and is each spot ready (ground under the pad, off the fairway, 20–50 yd behind the back tee, nothing overhead, a tips drive on 40 m of fairway) | seconds |
| `node tools/review/relief.mjs <course ...\|all>` | What the ground does on each hole: walks the playing line (height every 5 m and across it), names the height form (table top, hillside bench, valley carry, stepped, big drop or climb; crest and run, hog's back, half-pipe, tee drop, perched green, dip in front, fall-away; ramp, level; par 3s: drop, uphill, across a gulf, level) and flags fewer than two bold holes, a form more than twice or twice running, more than three plain par 4s and 5s, more than two tee drops, more than one drop par 3, and landing areas that all lean alike. Reads Halcyon Ring from `tools/halcyon/scene.mjs`. A hole with a `line` is walked along it, straight or not (before 12 November a straight hole was walked through its first safe target instead, which read the Strangler's table top as a ramp) | seconds |
| `node tools/review/height-forms.mjs` | Redraws `docs/height-forms.svg`, the height forms as side profiles, each a ground function `relief.mjs` names; `FORMS` is the worked data | seconds |
| `node tools/review/spread.mjs <course ...\|all>` | Do the holes sit in the middle of every dial? Measures each green's area, back-tee yards by par, each fairway's mean width, bunkers by each green and along each fairway (and the biggest), and each hole's bend; flags a dial whose holes cluster (greens, lengths, widths, bunker counts, bends), a nine that touches the edge of real golf on fewer than three dials, and a nine with no hole (or more than two) past real golf. `docs/COURSE-DESIGN.md` "Dials". Like `relief.mjs`, a hole with a `line` is measured along it | a minute |
| `node tools/review/mock/sample.mjs <course>` then `python3 tools/review/mock/book.py ...` | Proposal mockups for letting the owner choose among hole changes: a summary table, then a landscape page a hole (profile now and proposed, plans now and proposed at one scale, the numbers). Rough edits of the real ground, not built geometry; see `mock/README.md` | about a minute a course |
| `node tools/review/forms.mjs` | Redraws `docs/fairway-forms.svg`, the twelve fairway forms built with the plan tools' `makeFairway` and named by `alike.mjs`; its `FORMS` list is the worked data for each | seconds |
| `node tools/review/session.mjs [course]` | What keeps a real session alive: the screen wake lock is asked for, a swing on the flyover plays, a swing during the flight is held with a message and not played from the next lie, the round is saved after a stroke, and after a reload the setup screen offers Resume (checked at 844x390 and 667x375) and puts the ball back | about 5 min |
| `python3 tools/review/sheet.py <course> [holes] [--views=tee,land,green]` | A contact sheet of the views.mjs renders on a white page, captioned. `--files a.jpg b.jpg ... --title=...` makes one of any images (a mood board of references, a before/after) | seconds |
| `node tools/review/seethrough.mjs <course> [holes] [--only=...]` | Holes in the world: each view with sky, clouds and fog off on magenta; counts what you can see through looking down (a dropped playfield cell, a missing wall, a chasm with no floor) and writes a mask with the gaps in red. Not for the Aerie (open sky below is the design) | about 1 min a view |
| `node tools/review/pick.mjs <course> <hole> <tee\|land\|green\|pose> <x> <y> ...` | What's at a pixel of a 1280x720 render: the meshes a ray meets (material, colour, texture, instance) and what the terrain says is there, or background where you see sky | under a minute |
| `node tools/review/perf.mjs <course> [holes] [--vs=<ref>] [--top=5]` | What a hole costs from its back tee: meshes, triangles, a real frame's time at 844x390, the heaviest meshes. `--vs` measures another commit (a temporary worktree) the same way and prints both | 1–2 min a hole |

Renders use software WebGL, so they are slow and share two CPUs: run the browser tools in the background,
one at a time, and don't run them next to a long test. If the bundled Chromium shows a black page, set
`CHROME=/usr/bin/google-chrome`.

Hollowmere's plan book (yardage plans, sketched views, the round-by-round review PDFs) was made with
`tools/hollowmere/` plus scratch scripts in the same spirit as these. An art pass uses sheet, seethrough,
pick and perf; the steps are in `docs/ART-PASS.md`. `play.mjs` is the physics player `quickplay.mjs` and `design.mjs` share (`makePlayer(COURSE).playHole(hole, terrain, teeSet, seed, { scale, first, firstAt, skip })`; `firstAt: 1` aims `first` with the second shot). `page.mjs` has the shared pieces: `openCourse`,
`viewPoses` (the three review views of a hole), `goHole`, `setPose`, `hideMarkers`.
