# The Reclaim: art brief

Written after the fact from Rhys's reference photos (`refs/`) and his picks for art pass 1 (10 November
2026), as the example for `docs/ART-PASS.md`. A course without photos gets one of these before its art
pass.

**Feel.** A city about twenty years after everyone left. Quiet, not spooky: nature winning, a warm haze,
morning sun breaking through between the towers, big fluffy clouds overhead.

**Palette** (the big surfaces first)

| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Brick | `#80564a` `#704e40` `#8c7864` | walk-ups and warehouses, dark red to tan stone |
| Concrete | `#969490` `#868684` `#928c82` | towers, slabs, columns, banks |
| Asphalt | `#5a5750` | streets, with grass up the cracks and dust |
| Foliage | `#2f4a20` `#3a5726` `#46622c` | trees and ivy, deep greens |
| Grass | `#8aa45c` `#b0b070` | tufts through the cracks, rough |
| Water | `#3c5650` | still, dark, green |
| Fog | `#d2cdbf` | warm haze; the far skyline fades into it |
| Cabs | `#d6b02a` | the yellow that catches the eye |

**Materials.** Brick facades a 3 m bay and a 3.5 m floor, tall windows with stone lintels and sills,
soot runs, moss on the ledges, a stone band every four floors, cornices. Concrete towers a 4 m bay and a
3.8 m floor, punched windows, panel joints, water stains, a panel missing here and there. Streets cracked,
buckled, with dust and leaf litter.

**Vegetation**, in even measure: ivy climbing whole walls from the street and hanging off broken tops;
full, deep-green trees, some in the street, some on roofs; grass and weeds through every crack and over
the rubble; shrubs at wall feet; cars grown over.

**Sky, light and weather.** Signature: a cool morning in high haze, sun about 17° up, so long shadows
down the streets and shafts of sun through the haze; a cumulus deck overhead. Overcast keeps a grey mist.

**Life and details.** Stopped cars and cabs, a few buses, lamp posts (some leaning), traffic signals,
street signs, fire escapes; two or three grazing deer a hole and one flock of birds. Not busy.

**Landmarks.** The El, the fallen tower on 1, the propped tower on 4, the stadium, the bridge and the
leaning clubhouse with its clock tower.

**Avoid.** Gore, zombies, anything spooky; saturated cartoon colour; clean new surfaces.

**References.** `refs/street-cabs.jpg` (an overgrown street of stopped cabs in hazy light),
`refs/tree-arch.jpg` (a great tree arching over a street), `refs/park-deer.jpg` (a park going wild, a
deer).

**Next pass, candidates.** The El's deck, the station's roof beams, car windows and more grow-over,
towering clouds, the far skyline, a great tree arching over a street.
