# Red Mesa: art brief

Researched for its first art pass (10 November 2026; no reference photos yet), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** Warm, vast and quiet: a desert course at 5,200 ft played across and between red-rock mesas at
golden hour, the kind of place St George's courses (Sand Hollow, Entrada, Wolf Creek) sit in: emerald
fairways laid over red sand, rock walls streaked rust, brown and salmon, and big views of buttes going
away into the haze. Monument Valley's buttes on the skyline; Zion and Escalante's varnished walls up close.

**Palette** (the big surfaces first)

| | Hex | What |

| Bleached bands | `#e8cfae` | pale layers, leached by rain |

| Red sand and earth | `#b8673e` `#c98552` | the floor, washes, bunker sand (red, not grey) |

| Sky | `#3f74b8` overhead to `#f2c28a` at the horizon | deep blue above, warm haze at golden hour |

**Materials.** Walls with horizontal strata and cross-bedding, a resistant caprock lip at the top, and
dark varnish streaks below every pour-off; a talus slope of broken blocks at every cliff foot; rims that
are ragged, not ruled lines; the far buttes and mesas with caprocks and talus too. The floor is red sand
with slickrock patches, ripples in the washes, and boulders that sit in the ground, not on it.

**Vegetation.** Sagebrush and blackbrush scattered thick over the native ground (instanced, like The
Reclaim's grass); juniper and pinyon dotted on the tops and benches; cottonwoods only down the washes and
by the falls; a few prickly pear and yucca near the tees. Fewer round cartoon trees.

**Sky, light and weather.** Signature: golden hour, light wind, clear. Low sun raking across the walls so
one side glows and the other falls into blue shadow; long shadows from every butte and hoodoo; a warm
dust haze toward the sun; a few high, lit cirrus.

**Life and details.** Ravens and a red-tailed hawk circling; a lizard on the tee rock (the Lizard tees);
the train on the trestle, the windmill and the mine (there now); Ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings in an
alcove on one wall, as a landmark; a rusting ore cart at the mine. A few, not busy.

**Landmarks.** Needle's Eye's arch, the hoodoos, the trestle and train, the far buttes on the skyline.

**Avoid.** Walls as smooth vertical wallpaper; mesas as boxes with ruled edges; grey sand; round
lollipop trees on desert ground; a flat white sky at golden hour.
