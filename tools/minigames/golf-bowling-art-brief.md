# Golf Bowling: the Toy Room: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** Shrunk in a kid's bedroom on a sunny afternoon. The most finished scene; a light polish only.

**What it could use.** A visible sunbeam through the window with dust in it reaching the lane; a rug with a
woven fibre texture at its real scale (×30, so each tuft is a few centimetres); softer contact shadows under the
toys; the warm bounce light from the floorboards.

**Avoid.** Anything that gets in the way of reading the pins.
