# Carnival Booth: the Tin Gallery: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** An old fairground shooting gallery on a sunny late afternoon: painted steel and plywood, worn by
decades of use, warm low sun, bulbs just coming on. It already reads well; this is polish.

**Palette**
| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Stripes | `#d8483a` with `#f3ead8` | the arch and walls |
| Painted sea | `#2f6fa8` `#7fb3d9` | the waves, chipped |
| Brass and gold | `#c9a13b` | the bell, bulbs' sockets |
| Planks | `#8a5e3c` | the stage floor |
| Sky | `#9ec4e0` to `#f2d2a2` low | late afternoon |

**Materials.** Painted steel and plywood with chips, rust spots and wear where hands go (1920s galleries from
Pacific Ocean Park: painted steel, chain-driven targets); bulbs with a warm glow and a halo.

**Light.** Lower, warmer sun with long shadows across the planks; bulbs glowing; the fairground beyond softer
in haze so the gallery stands out.

**Avoid.** Fresh, flat colours with no wear; noon light.
