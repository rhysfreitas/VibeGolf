# Wedge Ladder: the Airfield: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** A vintage grass airfield at sunrise, the morning of a fly-in: low gold light, long shadows, mist
lying over the grass, balloons going up in the distance, dew on everything. Calm and bright.

**Palette**
| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | `#f6c48a` low to `#8fb4d8` high | warm glow at the horizon round the sun |
| Mist | `#f0e2cf` | low, in the hollows and over the far grass |
| Runway | `#4a4a4c` `#5d5c5a` | weathered asphalt, patched, lighter where worn |
| Markings | `#ece8de` | threshold bars, centreline, the big numbers |
| Grass | `#5f8a3c` mown, `#7b8f45` long | stripes near the strip, longer beyond |
| Trees | `#3f5a33` | a line with depth, hazier with distance |
| Hangar | `#8c9aa3` | corrugated, with a painted number |

**Materials.** Asphalt with seams, tar-banded cracks, tyre marks at the touchdown zone and lighter wear down the
middle; threshold "piano keys"; grass mown in stripes by the strip; a windsock, a fuel pump, tie-down ropes.

**Light.** Sun low on one side (about 8°), golden; long shadows from the signs, the plane and the tower; the
runway catching a glint toward the sun; mist glowing where the sun is behind it.

**Avoid.** A huge flat dark runway filling the bottom of the view (today); cold white light.

**References.** [Flying circus at dawn (Mark Alan Andre)](https://mark-andre.squarespace.com/blog/2016/8/20/the-flying-circus?format=amp),
[Shuttleworth's grass field and old aircraft](https://targeta.co.uk/shuttleworth2016.htm),
[Hot-air balloon photography](https://expertphotography.com/hot-air-balloon-photos).

**Rhys's picks (11 November 2026).** All seven games, worst first. A real sunrise: low gold sun, long shadows, mist on the grass, a weathered runway with markings, closer balloons.
