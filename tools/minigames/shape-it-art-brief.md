# Shape It: the Launch Bay: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** A working spaceship's hangar, clean and a little industrial, opening onto a dazzling view of
space. Concept-art hangars read through a clear module (ribs and bays repeated), two or three colours a
surface with hazard paint and markings, blue-tinted light in a faint haze, practical lamps plus light that
picks out shapes, steam and particles for life.

**Palette**
| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Hull plating | `#3a414c` `#55606e` | walls and deck, in panels |
| Hazard paint | `#e0a526` with `#1d1f24` | edges of the door, the launch slots |
| Lane lights | `#4fd2ff` | chasing toward the door |
| Warm practicals | `#ffb870` | a few lamps in the bays, so it isn't all blue |
| Space | `#05060c` with nebula `#3b2a6b` `#1e4f7a` | deep black, a soft nebula and dense stars |
| Planet | `#c98a4a` with rings `#e2c79a` | the ringed gas giant (there now) |

**Materials.** Deck plates about 2 m square with seams, scuffs and tyre marks; ribbed walls with pipes,
vents and lit signs (bay numbers, "LAUNCH"); a gantry or cranes overhead; ships with engine glows.

**Light.** Cool fill in a thin haze (light shafts from the overhead lamps), the door's force field glowing at
its edges, the space beyond brighter than now: a nebula, a dense starfield, the asteroid belt catching sun.

**Avoid.** Dark plain boxes for walls; a black rectangle where space should be; everything one blue.
