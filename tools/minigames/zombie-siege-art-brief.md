# Zombie Siege: Dead Farm at night: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** Spooky, not gory: a moonlit farm you can read all the way to the cornfield, warm firelight at your
fence, cold silver moonlight and rolling fog out where the horde comes from. Think of a moonlit prairie photo
and a horror film's night exterior: the moon as a high, far key light, smoke for depth, ground lights glowing
through it.

**Palette**
| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Moonlit grass | `#3d4a52` `#56636b` | the field: silvery grey-blue, never black |
| Trampled dirt | `#4a4038` | paths and the ground at the fence |
| Corn | `#5b5a3a` `#3a3b2a` | dry stalks, catching the moon on their tops |
| Fog | `#9aa8b8` (lit `#d8e0ea`) | in sheets, brighter where a floodlight crosses it |
| Firelight | `#ff9a3c` | barrels, the tractor, jack-o'-lanterns: warm pools on the ground |
| Moon | `#e8edf2` with halo `#7d8ca0` | huge and low, with a soft halo and craters |
| Sky | `#0f1824` to `#26364a` at the horizon | deep blue-black, lighter round the moon |

**Materials.** Grass with crop rows running away from you (they lead the eye to the cornfield and give depth);
dirt worn at the fence; weathered barn boards; headstones with moss; the fence posts lit warm on the near side.

**Light.** Real moonlight is silvery grey; films push it blue. Here: a cool, slightly blue moon key from behind
the field (rim light on the horde and the corn), warm fire pools near you, floodlight beams as soft volumes
with dust, fog in several layers so beams show where they cross it. The ground near you must read: today the
bottom half of the view is black.

**Life and details.** Bats across the moon, crows on the fence, fireflies near the fence, sparks from the power
line (all there or easy). A few, not busy.

**Avoid.** A black foreground; a flat white fog band; hard-edged beam cones; gore.

**References.** [Moonlit fog and hoar frost, Iowa](https://stormandsky.com/2011-12-9) (silver fields under fog),
[Moonlight on the prairie](https://amazingsky.net/2015/08/03), [The colour of moonlight](https://neiloseman.com/tag/night-shooting/)
(silvery grey in life, blue in films; smoke for depth, ground lights behind ridges glowing through it),
[5 ways to shoot night exteriors](https://www.indepthcine.com/videos/moonlight-lighting).
