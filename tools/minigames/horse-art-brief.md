# Golf HORSE: the Cul-de-sac: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** A cosy suburban cul-de-sac just after sunset, the blue hour of a real-estate twilight photo:
deep blue sky, warm windows and porch lights, the court under floodlights. Lived-in, friendly.

**Palette**
| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | `#1d2f55` overhead to `#e6a36a` at the horizon | blue hour with the last glow |
| Windows | `#ffcf8a` | warm, some with curtains, one TV flicker |
| Siding | `#c9c2b4` `#9fb0b8` `#c7a98a` | varied houses |
| Roofs | `#4a4648` `#5a4a40` | asphalt shingles |
| Lawns | `#3f6b34` | mown, darker at the edges |
| Street | `#2e3034` | asphalt with a light sheen under the lamps |

**Materials.** Gabled houses with shingled roofs, garage doors, porches and windows with frames; driveways,
mailboxes, hedges, flower beds, a few parked cars and bikes; trees with real canopies, not balls.

**Light.** Warm pools from porch lights and street lamps on lawns and drives; the floodlights softer (today they
glare); windows lit warm against the blue.

**Avoid.** Box houses; lollipop trees; dark empty street.

**References.** [Twilight real-estate photography (Redfin)](https://www.redfin.com/blog/twilight-photography-and-listing-your-home/),
[Twilight photography tips](https://expertphotography.com/twilight-real-estate-photography),
[Porch and door, night](https://gdanmitchell.com/2017/01/18/porch-and-door-night).
