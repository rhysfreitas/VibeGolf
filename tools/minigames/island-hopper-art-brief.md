# Island Hopper: the Aerie's sky world: art brief

Researched for the mini games' first art pass (11 November 2026; no reference photos), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`.

**Feel.** The same world as the Aerie course, so it should look like it: a golden morning over a bright sea of
cloud, pale quartz-sandstone islands banded amber, wind-bent pines, cumulus standing round the horizon
(`tools/aerie/art-brief.md` is the full brief; Rhys picked its direction).

**Today.** The islands are pale, unlit white cliffs with a smooth grey tint; the start island is a big smooth
green dome that fills the bottom of the view; the sky and the cloud sea are one grey-white. None of the Aerie's
art pass reaches it (the scene is its own file, `sky-islands.js`).

**What it needs.** The Aerie's light preset, its rock shading (bands, fractures, darker undersides), its pines
and cloud banks, a mown tee on the start island with a rim of rock, the targets' islands with fairway, rough
and a green each, and the cloud sea lit from above.

**Avoid.** A grey horizon; every island the same shape; flat white cliffs.
