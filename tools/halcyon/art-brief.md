# Halcyon Ring: art brief (pass 1)

Written 12 November 2026 from research (no reference photos from Rhys yet, beyond the mood he described in the
course brief: green parkland, fields and woods on the floor, lakes and rivers, white modern villas on terraces, the
ground curving up and over, great arms rising to a hub, glass along the rim, Earth and black space beyond). Decided
earlier: a pristine garden colony, the first tidy, built course; late light; the air handling's spinward breeze as
the wind. Make it our own station, not the film's.

## The feel

Calm, rich and impossible. An estate park kept perfect by people who could build a world, laid on a floor that
climbs away into the haze. The one thing that never stops being strange is the space: stars by day through the glass
at your feet and in the walls, Earth hanging in the west wall. Everything man-made is white and precise; everything
grown is lush and soft; the light is low and golden.

Desaturate a touch: low sun on white turns everything salmon and blinding (The Reclaim's lesson). White faces get a
slightly warm grey so a face square to the sun stays below pure white.

## Materials

- **Lawns**: the ground shader's `RING` branch: mowing stripes along each hole's line on fairways (6–8 m), wider
  park stripes on the rough, a darker ring under each clump of trees (shade and leaf litter), clover patches.
- **Terrace walls**: limestone ashlar, 0.6 m courses, a coping band 0.4 m along the top, a dark line of shadow under
  it; balustrades along the lips that people stand at (the 5th's balcony, the 9th's amphitheatre).
- **Villas**: white rendered walls, flat roofs with deep overhangs, long bands of glass (2.8 m floors), terraces
  with planters stepping down the slope; a warm light inside the glass. Three or four types repeated with tone.
- **The clubhouse**: the biggest villa, a long white pavilion with a colonnade and a terrace over the Great Window.
- **Glass**: the floor windows framed in a white kerb with mullions every 6 m; the rim walls' glass panes between
  ribs (5 m wide every 120 m) and horizontal mullions; through all of it, black space with stars, Earth to the west,
  the Moon to the east. A faint reflection of the haze at a low angle, never a grey sheet.
- **The spoke**: white, tapering, faceted legs rising out of frame toward the hub, a ring of lights where they meet
  the ground, an access door at the foot.
- **Water**: the lake and canal still, reflecting the walls and haze, a clipped grass edge to the water (no rocks).

## Vegetation

An English landscape park (Capability Brown) with formal gardens by the houses:
- **Clumps** of 3–9 big trees on the lawns (London plane, oak, cedar of Lebanon with its flat tiers), grazed-looking
  undersides at 2–3 m; **belts** of woodland along the rims and between holes; specimen trees alone on knolls.
- **Avenues** of poplar or cypress along the aqueduct and the tram line.
- **Formal gardens** only round villas and the clubhouse: clipped box parterres, low hedges, flower beds in lines
  (not blotches), topiary cones by doors, roses on the walled garden's walls (the 2nd).
- No shrubs in the play corridors; every tree off the lines as the layout has them.

## Sky, light and weather

- **The signature: late light.** A low golden sun raking across the ring, long shadows across the lawns, white
  faces lit warm on one side and blue on the other.
- **Where the light comes from** is the open question (see the picker): an open top with black space above the
  walls and the sun in through the east glass; or a glazed roof lit by a mirror band along the hub; or the haze as
  now, warmer.
- **The view up the ring** thickens into warm haze with distance (the reverse of Earth: the far floor rises out of
  the haze rather than sinking into it, Syd Mead's "inverse perspective").
- **Wind**: the air handling's spinward breeze; leaves stir, flags fly at the villas.

## Life and details

A few, never busy, all off the lines of play: swans on the lake, deer under the clumps, swallows, flags on the
villas, the tram gliding along its line, lit windows, a boat on the lake, statues or a temple in the park. Nothing
plays except trees.

## Landmarks

- **The spoke** (the 5th, 6th and 7th tees and more).
- **Earth through the west glass** (the 3rd, 4th and 9th).
- **The clubhouse** over the Great Window (seen up the ring from the early holes; played to at the end).

## Avoid

- Copying the film's station: its five fairings, its Beverly Hills grid, its name.
- Grey: the blockout's grey walls and grey windows. Glass shows space; structure is white.
- Pure white or saturated colours in low sun; graph-paper mullions (keep the glass's grid at its real size).
- Clutter: no sci-fi greebles on the lawns, no busy signage. It's an estate.
- Anything that suggests a ruin or wilderness: this is the one tidy course.

## References

- NASA Ames 1975 space settlement study, the Stanford torus and O'Neill cylinder paintings by Don Davis and Rick
  Guidice: green meadows and villages on the floor, the land curving up and over, rivers and bridges, mirrors
  bringing the daylight in. [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/space-colony-art-from-the-1970s/),
  [NSS: Stanford torus](https://nss.org/stanford-torus-space-settlement/), [NSS: O'Neill cylinder](https://nss.org/o-neill-cylinder-space-settlement/).
- [The Avery Review, "Ground into Sky"](https://averyreview.com/issues/6/ground-into-sky): the paintings as pastoral
  frontier meets suburb; no open sky, the land overhead; Syd Mead's "inverse perspective".
- [Gizmodo, designing Elysium](https://gizmodo.com/designing-elysium-all-the-secrets-of-neill-blomkamps-1069787673): an
  estate layout on a torus, lakes and rivers to balance the ring, the atmosphere as a strip rising above the ground.
- [National Trust, how to spot a Capability Brown landscape](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/discover/history/gardens-landscapes/how-to-spot-a-capability-brown-landscape):
  clumps, belts, cedars and planes, the serpentine lake with grass to its edge, temples, the Palladian bridge, the ha-ha.
- Kew's Palm House ([Grace's Guide](https://gracesguide.co.uk/Palm_House,_Kew_Gardens)): white ironwork ribs and
  curved glass, the model for the rim walls' frame.
- [Jewel Changi (Safdie Architects)](https://www.safdiearchitects.com/projects/jewel-changi-airport): a garden under a

  white villas over terraced gardens and cypress avenues.

## Rhys's picks (12 November 2026)

Asked in the picker, with `halcyon-light-options.jpg` (three skies painted over the 1st tee's render):
- **Light and sky: open to space.** Black sky and stars above the walls and in the glass, a low golden sun in
  through the east glass, long shadows. (Not a glazed roof under a mirror band, not the golden haze.)
- **Buildings: white modern villas** (his original mood): flat roofs, deep overhangs, long glass bands, planted
  roof terraces; white ribs, limestone terrace walls.
- **Planting: English landscape park**: clumps of planes, limes and cedars on striped lawns, woodland belts, grass
  to the water.
- **Details: garden follies, signs of people, station traffic** (not the wildlife).

## What pass 1 built (ring-world.js, main.js)

- The sky: a dome of black space and stars over the open ring, the air thin and blue a little above the floor and
  the warm haze at it (`skyDome`); the ring's sun fixed in the station, low (8°) through the east glass, a hot disc
  and corona in the glass shader (`uSun`, the Moon gone); the light preset warm and raking with a weaker, cooler
  fill (black space overhead); the walls dark glass with a cool sheen; the floor glass mirrors the black sky, so the
  windows read as holes into space; Earth with deeper oceans and swirled cloud. The floor is drawn 6 km round the
  ring either way on every quality, so it always climbs out of the top of the frame (a cut showed as a hard edge
  against the stars).
- Villas (`villa()`): slabs with deep overhangs, glass bands between piers (unlit dark panes, about a quarter lit
  warm), upper floors stepping back to planted roof terraces, a core behind the glass; the clubhouse with a
  colonnade under a deep roof; the hole's own villas (they play) drawn the same way over their boxes.
- Trees: planes and limes as three lumpy blobs, willows wide and light, cedars of Lebanon in flat dark tiers (about
  one in eight), poplars as columns; all on trunks, instanced.
- Follies (`COURSE.ring.follies`, placed by `emit.mjs` 95 m or more off every line): rotundas and temples on the
  lawns, obelisks, statues before villas, fountains (a big one on the clubhouse's forecourt) with flickering jets.
- Signs of people: flags on villas and three on the clubhouse, stirring in the breeze; lit windows; the tram (two
  white cars) gliding along its line; a sailing boat drifting up and down the lake.
- Station traffic: ships drifting past beyond the rim glass with blinking lights (in the glass shader), red beacons
  blinking on the spoke, two drones circling over the park well off the line.
