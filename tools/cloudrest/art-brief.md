# Cloudrest: art brief (art pass 1)

From Rhys's four reference pictures (kept in the session at `tools/cloudrest/out/refs/`, not in git): a
Tiger's Nest-style monastery on a forested crag above a green valley; a misty stone pinnacle hung with pines
between pale cliffs; and two of a cliff temple, halls and galleries set into a buff stone face under an
overhang, carved figures in niches, green on every ledge and mist below. Researched alongside them: Paro
Taktsang in Bhutan (the monastery), Huangshan (granite, crooked pines out of cracks, the sea of clouds) and
Zhangjiajie (pale quartz-sandstone pillars with pines on their ledges). Make it our own: the mood of a cliff
temple above the clouds, nothing from the show's design, no buildings hanging upside down under a lip.

## The feel

High, quiet and green. Late afternoon, the mist lifting: you play along the tops of two great pale cliffs,
looking down on a sea of cloud with pinnacles standing out of it, and across at a monastery in the far wall
that catches the low sun all day. Wet stone and pines, not bare mountain. Lived in but peaceful.

## Materials

- **Cliffs** (the ground shader's `CLOUD` rock, on every face of the field, the world map and the far map):
  buff-grey stone in tall slabs. Vertical joints every 4–9 m wandering a little, horizontal ledges every 12–20 m
  with a shadowed lip and a band of moss on top, dark water streaks running down from each ledge and joint,
  pale lichen on dry faces. The pattern must read at 300 m and at 30 m: fade the fine joints with distance so
  far walls don't turn into a grid. Faces are drawn from the ground itself, so no slabs: the blockout's flat
  ribbon faces get the same rock and are broken in plan (a wandering foot, buttresses) so they don't read as a
  fence.
- **Monastery** (the golden hall, the guesthouse, the bell tower, the lamp hall, the halls in the alcove):
  Bhutanese: whitewashed masonry walls with a slight batter, a maroon band under the eaves, dark carved timber
  windows (storeys about 3.5 m, windows about 1.6 m wide, grouped in threes on the upper floors), timber
  balconies, broad low roofs with deep eaves on short timber brackets, red (dark lacquer) roofs on most, gilded
  on the golden hall, a small gilded finial on each ridge. Canvas facade textures at real scale, one texture
  for many buildings with vertex tones.
- **Stupa**: whitewashed, a stepped base, a dome, a gilded spire of rings.
- **The alcove's back wall**: halls set into the rock under the roof, a gallery along them, two or three tall
  figures carved in niches in the stone between (seated, weathered, mossy; no faces to read at distance).
- **Paving** in the courtyard and on the kerbs: large worn flags about 1.2 m, grass in the joints.
- **Tea terraces**: rows of rounded dark-green bushes about 1.2 m wide along the contours, earth paths between.

## Vegetation

- **Pines** like Huangshan's: crooked trunks out of cracks, flat umbrella pads of needles, leaning out over
  the drop, on every ledge, lip and pinnacle top; straighter and denser on the tops back from the edges.
- **Moss and ferns** on every ledge band of the faces, thicker where it's wet (by the falls, the cleft, the
  alcove); **vines** hanging down the faces from the lips in curtains here and there.
- **Ground cover** on native ground: tussock grass and low shrubs (scrub as now), patchy.

## Sky, light and weather

- Signature: late afternoon, sun low in the west (about 12° up), warm side light, a light wind, mist.
- **The sea of clouds** at 150 m: a soft, billowing top lit warm where the sun reaches it, cool blue-grey in
  the cliffs' shadows, fading softly where it meets the rock (no hard line at the cliff foot), drifting slowly.
- **Wisps** drifting along the gorge between the pinnacles and up the faces, faint, never over the play.
- High thin cloud and a warm haze; the far mountains as soft blue silhouettes beyond the gorge's ends.

## Life and details

A few, off the lines of play (26 m or more), nothing within 14 m of a tee:
- Prayer flags in the five colours strung on lines between poles and rocks: across the cleft, up the pass on
  the 8th, by the cairn, along the monastery's paths.
- The monastery lived in: smoke from a chimney or two, butter lamps in the windows at dusk, prayer wheels by
  the courtyard, a bell in the tower.
- The rope bridge and the waterfall thread the layout drew as scenery.
- Birds: a pair of cranes or a lammergeier wheeling over the gorge; swifts along the faces.

## Landmarks

The monastery's golden roof (from 1, 2, 5, 6, 9), the Needle (from 3, 4, 5), the white stupa (5, 6).

## Avoid

Pure white walls and saturated red in the low sun; cone pines; flat-colour cliffs; striped fence-like faces;
a hard flat mist plane with a seam at the cliffs; anything that reads as the show's design; buildings under a
lip; scenery on a line of play or over a tee.

## What art pass 1 built

- **Cliffs** (`cloud-world.js` faces, main.js `CLOUD` rock): every edge over open air drops a face in rows (lip,
  then 7, 24, 55, 110 m down) that wander in and out with a world noise growing with depth, so walls stand as
  buttresses and bays. The stone: slabs between wandering joints, each a tone of its own, ledges every 12–20 m with
  a shadowed lip and moss on top, water streaks under the ledges, lichen, big bays and dark gullies along long walls,
  wetter toward the mist; grass over the lip; any ground steeper than about 37° is stone. Stone and grass fade into
  the cloud's colour over the last 26 m above it, so the cliffs meet the cloud softly.
- **Pinnacles**: drawn as irregular columns from the emitter's outlines (`COURSE.cloud.pinnacles`, the same noise as
  world.mjs), fluted, swelling with depth, under a grassy crown; the 10 m map made them square blocks.
- **The alcove**: its two walls as stone walls from below the floor into the roof (`COURSE.cloud.fins`), its roof
  as slabs of the same stone with a thick broken lip and hanging lumps (the 10 m map drew the walls as cones).
- **The sea of clouds**: a base sheet whose billows bump its normal, lit gold on their sunward sides and toward the
  sun, cool blue-grey in the troughs and the cliffs' shadows, and two patchy layers over it, all drifting.
- **Pines**: Huangshan pines (a crooked five-sided trunk, flat lumpy pads, three shapes plus one growing out of a
  face) leaning toward the nearest drop, on the plan's spots, the pinnacles' crowns and out of the faces at the
  ledges; vine curtains hug the faces from some lips in 30 m runs, their tips ragged.
- **Monastery**: a canvas facade at 10 m by 4.4 m storeys (the carved window on the top storey, small windows below,
  some panes lit warm), a stone plinth, the maroon band, a dark timber gap, low hipped roofs with deep eaves (red, the
  golden hall gilded with a raised lantern), finials, the bell tower's open belfry and bell; the lamp hall hung on the
  cliff's face below the courtyard (scenery: it was a solid box at mist level); the white stupa with its stepped base,
  dome and gilded spire; the pilgrim stair in steps; the cairn as stacked stones; the courtyard walls whitewashed with
  a maroon coping; the painted halls as three galleries with two seated figures in niches between; the prayer-wheel
  house's row of wheels; the rope bridge slung between its anchors with hand ropes.
- **Tea**: hedges along the contours (every 1.8 m of height) 30 m or more from the play and off fairways and greens,
  so a ball in the tea is never hidden; the ground shader paints the same rows nearer the play.
- **Details**: prayer flags that flutter (strings fanning from the cairn's mast, along the bridge, between the
  golden hall and its neighbours, banners on poles round the stupa), small chortens by the sites, chimney smoke,
  the thread of waterfall at the cleft's head on the 2nd, two lammergeiers wheeling over the gorge, swifts along a
  face. All scenery, 26 m or more off every hole's play (`COURSE.cloud.play`) and clear of the tees.
- **Cost** (perf.mjs against c2a0948, software): +34–47% frame time, +36–39% meshes, +40–60% triangles.

## Fixes after Rhys's look (16 November 2026)

Rhys: "something off with the shadows. A bunch of tree shadows that don't really make sense ... a lot of the cliff stuff is very jagged."

- **Shadows.** At golden hour's 8.5° a pine's shadow is about 7 times its height, so pines 26 m off the line (and behind the camera) laid streaks 80 m long across the fairways. Cloudrest's golden sun now stands at 20° (shadows about 2.7 times the height), still warm and low.
- **Cliff outlines.** The faces dropped straight down from the map cells' edges, a staircase (2 m in the field, 10 m on the world map) that read as stripes when lit. The top row is still the ground's edge; from 6 m down each corner follows the outline smoothed over 1.3 cells (`facePt`), so the walls curve. Vines and the cliff pines sit on the same surface.
- **Shading.** Faces and the alcove's fins take welded smooth normals (`weldNormals`) instead of one per triangle.
- **Facets up the slopes.** Inside the hole's box the 10 m map no longer draws over the playfield (its chords cut up through any curved slope as big flat facets, the mountainside on the 1st).
- **Hairline stripes.** The stone pattern ran along the wall's tangent from its normal; on a curved wall that coordinate swings by hundreds of metres. It is now read along x and along z and blended by facing (`cloudStone`).

Cost: no change in triangles, +0–2% frame time.
