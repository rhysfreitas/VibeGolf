# The Lost Valley: art brief (pass 1)

Written 12 November 2026 from research (no reference photos from Rhys). Decided in the course brief: a hidden valley
walled in by the cliffs of a table mountain (a tepui, like Roraima and Auyán-tepui, where Conan Doyle set *The Lost
World*), a golden afternoon after rain, a braided river, a swamp, a tar seep, the Great Falls, long-necks and
pterosaurs, an expedition's camp. Real places pushed past real: the Guiana Highlands' rock, water and cloud forest,
with Jurassic plants on the floor.

## The feel

Discovery. The first people to stand here in a hundred million years. The walls are so tall the valley feels like a
room with the sky for a roof; everything is wet and shining because it rained an hour ago, and the sun is back,
low and gold, coming in over the west rim. Water everywhere: falls off the walls breaking into mist before they reach
the bottom, a river the colour of tea, puddles in the meadow. The course is the only tidy thing in it, mown by the
expedition.

Keep it a little desaturated: wet things go darker and glossier, not greener. The only clean, bright green is the
course.

## Materials

- **The walls**: horizontal bedding every 2–6 m, vertical joints every 10–30 m that open into chimneys and
  fluting; overhangs every few beds. The black crust in vertical streaks 2–8 m wide down every water line and below
  every ledge; the pink-red shows on fresh faces and where a slab has fallen; grey on the dry tops. Moss and
  bromeliads on the ledges, a dark seep line under each one.
- **The talus**: blocks 1–5 m below the walls under cloud forest.
- **The fern meadow**: fronds 0.3–0.8 m in clumps, horsetail stands 1–1.5 m along wet ground, puddles 1–4 m in the
  low spots, catching the sky.
- **Mud and the dig pits**: wet and glossy, footprints of long-necks (0.8–1 m) along the swamp edge.
- **The river**: braided channels over gravel bars, the bars pale with pebbles; tea-brown water going black in the
  pools, white riffles where it runs fast, foam lines at the bends.
- **Tar**: still and glossy with slow bubbles, a crusted edge 1–2 m wide, bones and a half-sunk tusk or rib cage.
- **The Great Falls**: a free fall that breaks up on the way down, into lacy ribbons and then spray, so the bottom
  third is mostly mist; a spray cloud over the plunge pool with a rainbow in the sun; wet black rock round it.

## Vegetation

- **The floor**: fern meadow, horsetails in the wet, cycads (stout trunks, a crown of stiff fronds), tree ferns,
  scattered conifers; no grass outside the course and almost no flowers (flowers came later).
- **The landmarks**: the giant monkey-puzzle on the 1st (bare grey trunk, candelabra arms turned up at the tips, a
  flat-topped crown); the Strangler on the 8th (a lattice of grey roots fused round a hollow where its host tree
  rotted away, the arch through its roots, a dense crown, aerial roots hanging).
- **The swamp**: bald-cypress-like conifers with buttressed bases, knees, hanging moss.
- **The talus and the ledge**: cloud forest, dense and dark, epiphytes, tree ferns at the edge.
- **The walls**: moss and bromeliads on the ledges, ferns in the cracks, hanging gardens round the falls.

## Sky, light and weather

- The signature: golden afternoon after rain. The sun about 25° up behind the west rim, so the east wall glows and
  the west wall is in its own shadow; long shadows across the floor.
- Clouds clearing: the cumulus deck breaking up, a band of cloud still caught on the far rim.
- Wet: rock, leaves and mud shine toward the sun; puddles mirror the sky.
- Mist rising off the jungle and lying over the river and the swamp; strands clinging to the walls.
- A rainbow in the Great Falls' spray (low sun behind you on the 9th).

## Life and details (a few, off the lines)

- A herd of long-necks on the floodplain (seen from 2, 3, 5 and 7), pterosaurs over the east wall and the Overlook's
  pteranodon (already built), smaller things in the swamp.
- The expedition: canvas tents and a hut at the camp, crates, a rope ladder, the rope bridge, the survey pole, lanterns
  (light pools painted in the shader, never real lights).
- Bones: the skeleton in the Bone Bluff, ribs in the tar.

## Landmarks

The Great Falls (seen from 6, 7, 8, 9), the giant monkey-puzzle (1, 2, 3), the Bone Bluff (3, 4), the Strangler (7,
8), Nest Falls on the east wall. The reveal is the 7th tee (the Overlook).

## Avoid

Palm trees and tropical flowers; a bright saturated jungle green; grass prairie on the floor; dinosaurs anywhere near
a line or charging at the player; fog or mist across the lines of play; clean grey "fantasy" cliffs (these walls are
streaked black and pink); more than the two real lights.

  by hematite.
- Live Science, [Mount Roraima, the lost world](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/geology/mount-roraima-the-lost-world-isolated-for-millions-of-years-that-indigenous-people-call-the-house-of-the-gods):
  near-vertical walls, crystal pools on top, falls off every side, a ring of cloud.
- Australian Geographic, [Angel Falls](https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/?p=17932): the water half gone to
  vapour by halfway down; a "golden river" below.
- Wikipedia, [Jasper Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasper_Creek_(Venezuela)): water over smooth red and black
  jasper; [Tepui](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Tepui): walls, talus forest, endemic summit plants.
- NASA Earth Observatory, [tea-coloured rivers](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/some-tea-with-your-river-88843):
  blackwater coloured by tannins.
- Wikipedia, [Strangler fig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strangler_fig): the root lattice round a hollow core.
- American Conifer Society, [Araucaria araucana](https://conifersociety.org/conifers/araucaria-araucana/): the old
  tree's bare trunk and upturned arms.
- NPS, [plants of the dinosaurs' world](https://npshistory.com/handbooks/natural/dino/dino3.htm): ferns, horsetails,
  cycads and conifers; no grass.

## Rhys's picks (13 November 2026)

- **Rock: Roraima**, chosen from in-game renders of three (Roraima, golden sandstone, green all over): pink-red
  quartzite in beds, black streaks down every water line, grey dry faces, moss on the ledges.
- **Plants, light and details: no preference**, so the recommended options: the Jurassic floor (fern and horsetail
  meadow, cycads, tree ferns, monkey-puzzles, swamp cypress, no grass outside the course); wet and clearing (puddles,
  wet rock, mist, a rainbow in the Great Falls' spray, cloud on the rim); proper long-necks and better bones (the camp
  as it was).
