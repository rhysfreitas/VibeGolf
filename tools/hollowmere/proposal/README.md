Branch `hollowmere-proposal`: `holes.mjs` carries the proposed geometry (1 The Adit as a fan, 8 Glowworm Grotto as a
funnel, tips ground on 2, 4, 6 and 7, the 8th's tips spot) on top of main's data-only commit ed57bc8. Nothing here is
on main until Rhys approves it; the approved parts are then moved onto main's `holes.mjs`.

Scratch tools used to build the proposal book (paths inside point at the session's scratch folders; adjust before
reuse): `grid-export.mjs` samples a module's holes in their frames, `nums.mjs` the forms and landings, `exp.mjs`
quick checks on the world-frame data, `spot.mjs` one tips spot, `tips-search.mjs` candidate tips spots,
`toworld.mjs` hole frame to world, `grid6.mjs` a text height map, `plan.py`/`book.py` the white-page PDF.
