# Hollowmere: art brief

Written for its second art pass (10 November 2026), per `docs/ART-PASS.md`. The first pass (the
lanterns, crystals, glowworms, giant mushrooms, the geode, the cenote, the baked lights) followed Rhys's
cenote and geode photos; this brief sets down that direction and what the next pass should add.

**Feel.** Wonder underground: old limestone mine caverns, dark and quiet, where the play is lit like a
stage: lantern pools, a lit green, sunlight falling through holes in the roof. The real-world models are
Son Doong's dolines (a jungle growing in a shaft of sun, mist in the light, ferns and moss where less light
reaches), the Waitomo glowworm grotto (blue-green points on a black ceiling, reflected in still water) and
the Yucatán cenotes (vines hung from a ragged opening, turquoise water).

**Palette** (the big surfaces first)

| | Hex | What |
|---|---|---|
| Limestone | `#6b665e` `#8f897d` | walls and roof, banded |
| Flowstone | `#c8b89a` | pale, wet-looking runs down the walls |
| Cave mud and rubble | `#3b352e` `#4f463b` | the floor off the play |
| Turf under light | `#5c8f34` | fairways and greens, only bright where lit |
| Moss and ferns | `#3f6b2a` `#5d8a36` | round the openings and the falls |
| Lantern | `#ffb35c` | warm pools along the play |
| Glowworms | `#6ff2e0` | blue-green points, the grotto's ceiling |
| Crystal | `#b78cff` | amethyst in the geode and the crystal cave |
| Still water | `#1d3f45` with reflections | the lake and the pools, dark and mirror-like |
| Sunbeam | `#fff1cf` | soft, misty shafts from the openings |

**Materials.** Limestone with banding and flowstone (there now); wet sheen near water and falls; mud
floors with puddles; old timber (props, trestle, rails) dark with age; mushroom caps with gills under them
and a faint glow.

**Vegetation.** Moss, ferns and hanging vines only where daylight reaches (the openings, the cenote), a
little doline jungle under the biggest shaft; elsewhere bare rock. Giant mushrooms in the grotto.

**Sky, light and weather.** Signature: midday, so the sun falls steeply through the roof openings. Soft,
misty beams with no hard edges, dust motes in them; the lit green and fairway as now; lanterns along
the play; everything else falls away to black.

**Rhys's picks (10 November 2026).** All four fixes: misty beams and real falls (pool, spray, mist),
Mirror Lake reflects, mushrooms and crystals with depth, turf that falls into the dark.

**Life and details.** Bats leaving through a shaft; drips and ripples in the pools; mine relics (rails,
carts, a winch, a lamp hook); glowworm threads hanging from the grotto's ceiling.

**Landmarks.** The waterfall in the Hollow, the geode, the cenote's opening, the Deep.

**Avoid.** Flat planes that read as cards (falls, beams, crystal facets); plastic-looking mushrooms; a
lake that doesn't mirror; neon turf in the dark.
